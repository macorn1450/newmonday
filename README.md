# newmonday.pictures

Site for New Monday Pictures, a New York documentary production company.

Everything served lives in `docs/` (GitHub Pages serves that folder):
- `docs/index.html` holds the page, its styles, and the film list (`WORK`) and chapters (`CHAPTERS`) near the bottom.
- `docs/media/` holds the hero loop, its poster frame and the chapter stills.

Films play from Vimeo. Videos limited to approved domains need newmonday.pictures on their allowed list.
